What Givezy does for your charity

Givezy is a South African giving platform. Donors find registered charities and give once or every month. You get the money in your own bank account and a dashboard to look after your donors.

For your donors

  • They find you by name, cause or area, or land straight on your page from a link, a QR code or a button on your website.
  • They give once or every month, from R30 a gift. They type their card on our payment provider's secure page. Givezy never sees or stores card details.
  • Every gift lands in one list in their Givezy account, with their s18A receipts ready at tax time.
  • Monthly donors can pause, restart or cancel themselves, any time, from their account.

For you

WhatWhat it means for you
Your own public pageYour photo, what you do, how donations are used, suggested amounts and a thank-you note. It goes public once your application is approved.
Donations and donorsEvery donation and every donor in one place, including gifts that reached you by EFT or cash, which you can record yourself.
Monthly donorsDonors can give the same amount every month until they cancel. You see who gives monthly, and when a card is declined.
Campaigns with a goalRaising for something specific, like winter food parcels or a new roof? Donors watch the campaign fill up.
Thank and update donorsPost photos and updates, send a monthly thank-you, and email news or an appeal. Givezy sends the emails, so you never handle donors' addresses.
Downloads for your bookkeeperDonations, donors and bank deposits as spreadsheet files (CSV), and an annual report for your board or AGM.
s18A receiptsIf you have s18A approval and sign our receipting mandate, Givezy issues and emails your donors' s18A receipts in your name, once a year.

Try it first, with sample data. The For charities page on the Givezy website has a link, "Try the dashboard with sample data". It shows the dashboard filled with made-up donations, so you can look around before you apply.

What it costs and how the money moves

Short version: listing is free, Givezy takes nothing from your donations, and the payment provider's card fee comes off each donation.

CostWhat you pay
Signing up and listingNothing. No sign-up fee and no monthly fee.
Givezy platform feeNone. Givezy charges no platform fee and no processing fee on donations.
Card feeOur payment provider charges a fee on each card payment. Each side pays it on its own share: your charity pays the fee on its donation, and Givezy pays the fee on any contribution a donor adds for Givezy. Your part comes off the donation before the money reaches your bank. The exact fee will be confirmed before going live.

How a donation reaches you

  1. The donor givesThey choose an amount (from R30, up to R1 000 000 online) and pay on our payment provider's secure page.
  2. The payment provider confirms itA gift only counts as paid once the provider confirms it. Until then it shows as waiting.
  3. The money goes to your bank accountThe provider pays your gifts into your own bank account in one deposit a day, with its fees taken off. Givezy never holds your donations.
  4. You match it to your bank statementYour dashboard's Deposits page shows each deposit, the gifts it is made of, each fee and what reached your bank, so it matches your bank statement line for line.

The donor's optional contribution to Givezy

On the give form, a donor may tick a box to add a separate contribution to Givezy (at least R10). It is unticked unless they choose it. It is not part of your donation, it is never on the s18A receipt, and it does not show on your dashboard.

Covering the card fee

Once the card fee is confirmed, donors will also see an optional box to cover it, so you receive the full gift. The s18A receipt is always for the gift only.

Gifts that don't come through Givezy

An EFT, cash, a debit order or a gift through another giving programme can be recorded on your dashboard (one at a time, or many from a spreadsheet). No card fee or contribution applies, and if the donor wants one, the gift goes on their yearly s18A receipt with their other gifts to you.

Before you start

Applying takes one sitting if your papers are ready. You can also save and come back any time: everything saves as you go.

Who can sign up

  • A registered NPO or PBO. The application asks for your NPO number or your PBO reference number (or both).
  • s18A receipts need s18A approval from SARS. Organisations without s18A approval are welcome too. Donors can still give; no tax receipts are issued.
  • The person who applies becomes the owner of your charity on Givezy. Choose someone who will stay involved, because some things only the owner can do (see section 5).

Documents to have ready

PDFs or clear photos (JPG or PNG), up to 4 MB each. You can upload straight from your phone.

DocumentWhen it's needed, and what it must show
Bank confirmation letter EveryoneA letter from your bank showing the account is in the organisation's name, with the account number and branch code. Donations are paid into this account.
PBO approval letterIf you give a PBO number: the SARS letter confirming your PBO status.
s18A approval letterIf you give an s18A reference number: the SARS letter that lets you issue s18A receipts.
Signed receipting mandateIf you give an s18A reference number. It authorises Givezy to issue s18A receipts in your name. We send you the mandate to sign; you upload the signed copy and type the date it was signed.
NPO registration certificate, other papersOptional. The upload list also takes these, if they help us check your registration.

Details to have ready

  • Your organisation: the name donors will see, your registered legal name exactly as on your NPO or PBO documents (it appears on s18A receipts), and your NPO number (it looks like 123-456 NPO), PBO reference and s18A reference, as they apply.
  • Registered address: the address on your registration documents. It also appears on s18A receipts.
  • A contact person: full name, email and, if you like, a phone number. We email this person about your application and your page.
  • Bank account: bank, account holder (the organisation's name), account number and branch code.
  • A phone with an authenticator app (see the next page).

How long the check takes. A person at Givezy checks every application by hand and reviews it within 2 working days of you submitting it.

Signing up, step by step

Yehuda will send you an invitation link. When you open it, your charity's name is already filled in.

Getting in

  1. Type your organisation's name and your emailOn the "List your charity" page, then press "Email me a code". There is no password: each time you sign in, Givezy emails you a code.
  2. Type the code from the emailYou can come back to your application from any device the same way.
  3. Agree to the termsTick that you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy and that you are 18 or older.
  4. Set up the second step on your phone (about two minutes)Givezy asks every charity user for a second step when signing in, because your dashboard holds donors' details. See the box below.

What is an authenticator app?

A free app on your phone that shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds or so. When you sign in, Givezy asks for the code that is on your phone right then. Someone who learns your email still can't get in without your phone.

Good choices are Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy. Install one, then on the Givezy screen: scan the QR code with the app (or type the key shown underneath), type the 6-digit code it shows, and press "Confirm and continue". You do this once. After that, each sign-in is the email code plus the code from the app.

Lost or changed your phone? Givezy can reset it once we've checked it's you, using your contact person's details.

The application: six short steps

A progress bar shows how many steps are done. You can skip a step and come back to it.

  1. Your organisationName donors will see, registered legal name, NPO, PBO and s18A numbers. About two minutes.
  2. Registered addressAs on your registration documents.
  3. Contact personWho we email about your application and your page. You can change it later.
  4. Bank accountWhere donations are paid. It must be in the organisation's name. It is stored encrypted, and only the last four digits are ever shown.
  5. DocumentsUpload the papers from section 3. A tick shows next to each one that's done.
  6. SubmitTick four declarations: you are authorised to act for the organisation; the information and documents are true and correct; you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy, including the section for charities; and you will use donors' information only to record donations and thank donors, keep it secure under POPIA, and never sell or share it. Then press "Submit for review".

Once submitted, it can't be taken back, and you can't change your details or bank account while we review them. Check the bank details twice.

What happens next

  1. We check it within 2 working daysA person checks your registration, bank letter and approvals, then emails your contact person. If something needs fixing, we say exactly what, and you can update the application and send it again.
  2. While you waitWrite your public profile, add a cover photo, and add your team (you can add people once your application is sent).
  3. You're approvedGivezy connects your charity to our payment provider so you can receive online donations (we do this for you). Your page opens to donors.
  4. Getting startedYour dashboard shows a short list that ticks itself off: add a cover photo, tell donors what you do and where gifts go, post your first update, and start a campaign if you're raising for something specific.

Monthly giving

  • Any donor can choose "every month". The same amount is collected by card on the same day each month, until they cancel.
  • Donors pause, restart or cancel it themselves. You can't change a donor's monthly donation, so nobody has to ask you.
  • When a card is declined, you see it on Monthly donations and under "Needs you this week".

The phone app

There is a Givezy phone app for donors, where they can find charities, see campaigns and read your updates. For now it is a test version on iPhone, through Apple's TestFlight, for the people testing Givezy. It is not in the app stores yet, and we can't give a date. While it is being tested, giving from the app opens our website.

A few ideas that cost nothing

  • Put the QR poster up where people already wait or gather.
  • Add the link to your email signature and WhatsApp status.
  • Post an update with a photo after each project, and send the monthly thank-you.

s18A receipts and SARS

Givezy prepares and sends your donors' s18A receipts in your name. You check the list first, and you submit your own IT3(d) to SARS.

What has to be in place

  • Your s18A approval, confirmed by Givezy from your SARS letter.
  • Your signed receipting mandate, with the date it was signed. Gifts paid before that date can't go on a receipt.

Only when both are in place can Givezy issue s18A receipts for you. If you get s18A approval after you join, send Givezy the SARS letter and the signed mandate and we set it up.

How the yearly run works

The SARS tax year runs from 1 March to the end of February. Receipts are issued once a year, after it ends: one receipt per donor for all their gifts to you that tax year, for the gift amounts only.

  1. The list appearsAfter the tax year ends, your s18A receipts page shows the donors who asked for a receipt, how many gifts, and how much.
  2. You check it and approveCheck the names and amounts. If a name looks wrong or someone is missing, don't approve yet: tell us. Until Givezy signs off, you can take your approval back.
  3. Givezy signs offWe check the list, then the receipts are issued and each donor gets theirs by email. Every receipt issued in your name is listed on your dashboard.

Donors who give before adding their details

An s18A receipt needs the donor's SA ID number (or a company's or trust's registration number) and address. A donor can choose "Add my details after paying" and add them later on the thank-you page or from their account. Until they do, that gift gets no receipt, and your list shows it as "details missing". For the tax year just closed, they can add details until 31 May.

Your IT3(d) return to SARS

Each charity submits its own IT3(d) to SARS; Givezy prepares the file for you. The owner goes to s18A receipts, then "SARS submission (IT3(d))", fills in your SARS details once (income tax reference number, legal form and registration number), chooses the tax year, and sees anything that must be fixed first.

  • Up to 50 receipts: download the capture sheet (a spreadsheet) and type it into the IT3-01 form on eFiling.
  • More than 50: download the SARS data file, which your accountant or a SARS-registered submitter sends to SARS.

Check your first submission with your accountant.

Not final yet: the s18A receipt wording is with our attorney, so receipts can't be issued for real until it is approved. The 31 May cut-off and parts of the IT3(d) file are still being confirmed with an accountant.

Privacy and security

Your dashboard holds people's names, emails and giving. This is what you see, what you don't, and how it is protected.

On your dashboardWhat you see
You seeDonors who gave to you: name, contact details, amount, their message, and whether they asked for an s18A receipt.
You never seeCard details (Givezy never sees or stores them either), any part of a donor's ID or tax number, donors' contributions to Givezy, or anything about other charities.
Anonymous donorsAnonymous on public pages only. You see their details, because you need them to record donations.
The one exceptionThe IT3(d) files for SARS hold donors' full ID numbers, because SARS requires them. Only the owner can download them, and on the page an ID shows only its last 4 digits.

How your account is protected

  • A second step on every sign-in: the email code plus the code from your authenticator app, for the owner and every admin.
  • Bank details: stored encrypted; only the last four digits are ever shown.
  • Changing the bank account after you've applied: this can't be done from the dashboard. Send Givezy a new bank confirmation letter. We check it, make the change, and email your contact person. Online donations pause until we have connected the new account.
  • Contact person: only the owner can change it. If the email or phone changes, we tell the old address too. We use these details to check it's really you before we reset a sign-in or change the bank account.
  • Downloads are limited to a few at a time and recorded, so we can see who downloaded what.

What we ask of you

  • Give dashboard access only to people who need it, and remove them on the Team page when they leave.
  • Use donors' information only to record donations and thank donors, keep it secure, and never sell or share it (you agree to this when you submit).
  • Keep downloaded files, especially the IT3(d) files, somewhere safe, and delete copies you no longer need.

Still with our attorney: the Terms, the Privacy Policy, and the privacy wording about the owner downloading donors' ID numbers for SARS.
