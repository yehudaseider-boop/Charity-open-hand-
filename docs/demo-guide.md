Hi,

Thanks for taking a look at Givezy. This is a working demo you can click around in. Everything in it is made up:
the charities, the donors and every rand. Nothing is charged and no emails go out, so you can't break anything
that matters. It all goes back to how it started every night.

**The link:** https://givezy-demo.vercel.app/demo

## Pick who you want to be

- **Thandi, charity owner.** Runs Northcliff Meals Fund. Start here if you run a charity.
  Have a look at: Donors (notes, tags, follow-ups and saved lists), Campaigns, Monthly donations,
  s18A receipts, Deposits, and Email donors.
- **Sipho, charity admin.** Same charity, helps Thandi, but can't change the team.
- **Lerato, Givezy admin.** What we see: new charity applications, receipt sign-off and the cause of the week.
- **David, donor.** His giving, his monthly donation, his s18A receipts and his tax pack.

You can also browse as a visitor and give to a charity. When you pay, you land on a pretend payment page where you
choose "paid" or "declined". No card details are asked for.

## Good to know

- Please don't type real details (your own email, ID number or bank details) into the demo. Other people trying it
  can see what you type until the nightly reset.
- Other people use the same demo at the same time, so something may have moved since you last looked.
- "Switch person" in the orange bar at the top takes you back to the four buttons.
- s18A receipts in the demo open as a page marked DRAFT.
